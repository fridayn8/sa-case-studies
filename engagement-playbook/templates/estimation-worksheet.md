# Template — Estimation Worksheet

**Stage:** [5 Proposal](../05-proposal-and-commercial.md), updated at every gate and at [9 Review](../09-review.md) · **Method:** [timeline and effort](../timeline-and-effort.md) · **Worked example:** [Case 01 estimate](../../case-01-example-corp/05-proposal-delivery/estimate-and-timeline.md)

## How to use

1. For each stage give three estimates in days: **O**ptimistic, **L**ikely, **P**essimistic. Calculate `expected = (O + 4 × L + P) / 6`.
2. Add **contingency** (20–30 %) as a separate line and say what it covers. Re-apply it to the remaining work at each gate.
3. Write the **assumptions** next to the numbers.
4. After each stage fill in **actual** effort and calendar time and the **delta**. After three engagements, copy your pattern into the **calibration** column and into your next estimate.
5. Estimates are **illustrative** until you have your own data.
