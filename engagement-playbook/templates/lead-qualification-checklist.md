# Template — Lead Qualification Checklist

**Stage:** [0 Pitch](../00-pitch.md) · **Worked example:** [Case 01 first contact](../../case-01-example-corp/00-pitch-discovery/01-first-contact.md#4-lead-qualification)

## How to use

1. Fill it in during or right after the first call, in the client's own words.
2. Mark every row ✅ (clear), ⚠️ (unclear, needs work) or ❌ (blocker).
3. A **blocker** means: reframe it with the client, or say no. Do not start discovery with an unresolved blocker.
4. Write the go/no-go decision and its reasons at the bottom; keep the sheet with the engagement.

## Mini-example

| Question | Answer | Assessment | Next step |
|----------|--------|:----------:|-----------|
| Budget range | "Around USD 5 000, I assumed that is what a good firewall costs" | ⚠️ | Test against a five-year view in the pitch |
| Deadline | "Before the summer holidays"; insurance renewal on 1 October | ⚠️ | Treat the insurance date as the constraint |
| Sponsor | The managing director, who also holds the budget | ✅ | Name in the discovery proposal |

**Decision:** Go for discovery only, with three conditions (budget tested openly, discovery time-boxed, all groups heard).
