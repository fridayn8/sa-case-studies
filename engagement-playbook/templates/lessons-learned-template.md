# Template — Lessons Learned

**Stage:** [9 Review](../09-review.md) · **Worked example:** [Case 01 lessons learned](../../case-01-example-corp/05-proposal-delivery/lessons-learned.md)

## How to use

1. Hold the session a few weeks after go-live, with the client's operations, the sponsor and the team. Make it **blameless**: describe causes in the process, not in people.
2. Start with the **goals** and their measured results, not with the deliverables.
3. Compare **estimate and actual** (use the [estimation worksheet](estimation-worksheet.md)).
4. For each lesson write: what happened, the evidence, the change for next time.
5. List the **debt** (postponed work, accepted risks, unvalidated assumptions) with owners and review dates.
6. Book a follow-up check (three to six months).
