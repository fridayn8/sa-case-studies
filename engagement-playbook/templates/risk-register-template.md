# Template — Risk Register

**Stages:** started in [1 Discovery](../01-discovery.md), maintained from [5 Proposal](../05-proposal-and-commercial.md) · **Worked example:** [Case 01 risk register](../../case-01-example-corp/05-proposal-delivery/risk-register.md)

## How to use

1. Start it in discovery with what you already see; review it **at every gate**.
2. Give each risk an ID, a clear sentence ("if X, then Y"), likelihood, impact, **an owner** and a mitigation. A risk with no owner is a worry.
3. Score = likelihood × impact (Low 1, Medium 2, High 3). Treat score 6 and above first.
4. Choose a response: **avoid**, **reduce**, **transfer**, or **accept** (accept needs a named person who accepts it).
5. Link risks to assumptions: an unvalidated assumption is a risk.
6. Keep a review log so that you can see how risks moved.
