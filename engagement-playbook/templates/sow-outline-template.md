# Template — SOW Outline

**Stage:** [5 Proposal and commercial](../05-proposal-and-commercial.md) · **Worked example:** [Case 01 SOW outline](../../case-01-example-corp/05-proposal-delivery/sow-outline.md)

> An outline for learning. A real statement of work needs review by someone qualified to give legal advice.

## How to use

1. Be specific about **scope, deliverables and acceptance**: "documented and tested" is not an acceptance criterion; "restored from backup within the RTO" is.
2. Put the **out-of-scope list** next to the scope. It prevents the argument later.
3. Add **client responsibilities with dates**, because delays are most often on the client side.
4. Describe **change control** before anyone asks for a change.

## Template

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| 1. Purpose and parties | What this covers, who signs, what it replaces |
| 2. Scope | In scope; who does the build; what the provider does not do |
| 3. Deliverables | ID, deliverable, acceptance criteria, requirement IDs |
| 4. Responsibilities | RACI for provider, client IT, partner, sponsor; client dependencies with dates |
| 5. Change control | Request, assess (design, cost, time, risk), decide (who), update, pay |
| 6. Commercial terms | Fee type, cap, invoicing points, expenses, payment terms, termination |
| 7. Assumptions | The assumptions the price depends on, each with a consequence if false |
| 8. Out of scope | A list, specific and unambiguous |
| 9. Acceptance and closure | What must be true for the work to be closed |
