# Template — Stakeholder Map

**Stage:** [1 Discovery](../01-discovery.md) · **Worked example:** [Case 01 stakeholders](../../case-01-example-corp/00-pitch-discovery/04-stakeholders-and-interviews.md#1-stakeholder-map)

## How to use

1. List everyone who is affected, decides, pays, operates or can block, including external parties (an insurer, a partner).
2. Rate **interest** and **influence** (Low, Medium, High). The high/high group is your core audience; the high-interest, low-influence group needs to be heard, not ignored.
3. Record the **fear** as carefully as the wish: fears become requirements and risks.
4. After interviews, add the conflicts you found, and use the map in the final workshop.
